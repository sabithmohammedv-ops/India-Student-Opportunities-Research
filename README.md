
# 🌍 Student Opportunities Research & Data Collection – India

### InAmigos Foundation | AI Data Analytics Internship

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on researching, collecting, and organizing information about volunteer opportunities, internships, fellowships, government initiatives, and social impact programmes available for students and young people across India.

The objective is to create a structured database that helps students discover opportunities to gain practical experience, develop professional skills, participate in community development, and contribute to society.

This research was completed as an individual project as part of my AI Data Analytics Internship at **InAmigos Foundation (IAF)**.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Research volunteer opportunities available across India.
- Identify internships and fellowships offered by different organizations.
- Explore government initiatives and youth development programmes.
- Discover social impact programmes related to education, healthcare, and community development.
- Collect eligibility criteria and application deadlines.
- Gather official application links from respective organizations.
- Organize the collected information into a structured Excel database.
- Develop research, data collection, and data organization skills.
